Scope and notes

  • Why no shared strip: the CEO's four L1s are the shared layer. Other executives see the CEO metric their L1s roll into, marked "shared · owned by", so numbers match in common meetings without a second list.
  • FY outlook is the latest-estimate comparison on every tile, not an L1 metric. EPS vs guidance sits as a strip above the CEO's cards and is owned by the CFO.
  • RGM sits with the CFO (signs off promotion budgets, owns margin). Sales sees the execution side: trade spend vs agreed terms and promotion compliance.
  • Inventory ownership: the COO's inventory days cover our own stock. Channel stock (distributors, retailers) is owned by the Chief Sales Officer and shown to the COO as context.
  • Evidence limits: operating KPIs (OTIF, perfect order, cost per case) rarely appear in company filings; they rest on SCOR, Gartner and BCG standards. Brand equity is tracked internally by CPGs but never published. CMO, Chief Sales Officer and COO incentive metrics are not publicly disclosed.
  • Out of scope: people, R&D pipeline, IT, sustainability, financing and M&A.
